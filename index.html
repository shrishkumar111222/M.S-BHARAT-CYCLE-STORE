Why Choose Us

Everything a cycle needs,
under one roof.

Buying a cycle is the easy part. What matters afterwards is whether someone nearby can service it, stock the right part and give you a straight answer. That is the shop we have built in Sherghati.

Bicycle Sales

A showroom floor of cycles from every trusted Indian brand — kids, ladies, mountain, road, hybrid and electric.

Repairs & Servicing

An in-house workshop with the right tools. Punctures and adjustments are usually done while you wait.

Genuine Spare Parts

Original chains, sprockets, tubes, tyres, brakes and bearings. No look-alike parts that fail in a month.

Advice from people who ride these roads

We size the cycle to the rider, not to the sale. Tell us where you ride — town roads, village tracks, school runs — and we will point you to the model that will still feel right in three years.

Bicycle Accessories

Helmets, locks, lights, pumps, bells, carriers, baskets, mudguards and bottle cages — all in stock.

Affordable Pricing

Honest, clearly-quoted prices. You are told the cost before the work starts, every single time.

Expert Guidance

Years on the shop floor means we size the cycle to the rider and recommend what actually suits your roads.

    WORKSHOP
    Repairs & Servicing

    A workshop, not just
    a counter.

    Every cycle we sell can be serviced here for as long as you ride it. Bring it in — most jobs are finished the same day.

    workshop.jpg
    Same Day Most Repairs

    • General diagnosis and repair for every part of the cycle — frame, chain, pedals, bearings and cables. Most jobs are finished the same day while you wait.

    • Spoke tensioning and rim truing on a proper wheel stand, so a buckled wheel runs straight again instead of rubbing the brake pads.

    • Pad replacement, cable renewal and caliper adjustment for V-brakes and disc brakes. We set the bite point where you want it.

    • Derailleur indexing and limit-screw setting so every shift lands cleanly, plus a check of the hanger alignment.

    • Puncture repair while you wait, and fitting of new tubes or tyres from stock in all common sizes.

    • Fitting for carriers, baskets, mudguards, lights, bells, locks, bottle cages, stands and child seats.

    • A yearly service package: full strip-down clean, re-greasing of bearings, cable replacement and a complete safety check.
